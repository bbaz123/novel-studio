## English

### Our pledge

We want participating in Novel Studio to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct. The full text — including the standards we expect, the
responsibilities of maintainers, and the enforcement guidelines — applies in full.
The summary below is a convenience, not a substitute.

**Expected:** be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences, give and
accept constructive feedback gracefully, focus on what is best for the project and
its users, and show empathy toward other community members.

**Not acceptable:** sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment,
publishing others' private information without permission, and any conduct that
would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

### Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages and code review — and also when an individual is officially representing
the project in public spaces.

### Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/bbaz123/novel-studio/security/advisories/new)
(mark it `[Conduct]`) or by contacting the maintainer [@bbaz123](https://github.com/bbaz123)
through GitHub. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially; the
reporter's privacy and safety are prioritised.

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, up to and including a
temporary or permanent ban from the project, following the enforcement guidelines
in the Contributor Covenant.

## 简体中文

### 我们的承诺

我们希望参与 Novel Studio 的每个人都拥有**不受骚扰**的体验，不因年龄、体型、残障、
民族、性别认同与表达、经验水平、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向而受到区别对待。

本项目采用 **[Contributor Covenant 2.1 版](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
作为行为准则，**完整正文（含我们期望的行为标准、维护者职责与执行指南）全部适用**。
下面的摘要只是为了方便阅读，不能替代正文。

**期望的行为**：尊重不同的观点与经历；得体地提出和接受建设性意见；以项目和用户
的最大利益为重；对其他社区成员保持同理心。

**不可接受的行为**：性化的语言或图像；挑衅、侮辱或贬损性评论；人身或政治攻击；
公开或私下骚扰；未经允许公布他人隐私信息；以及在专业场合中显然不当的其它行为。

### 适用范围

适用于本项目的所有场合——issue、pull request、discussion、提交信息与代码审查——
也适用于个人在公开场合正式代表本项目时。

### 报告与执行

如遇辱骂、骚扰或其它不可接受的行为，请开一份
[私下安全报告](https://github.com/bbaz123/novel-studio/security/advisories/new)
（标题标注 `[Conduct]`），或通过 GitHub 联系维护者 [@bbaz123](https://github.com/bbaz123)。
所有报告都会被及时审阅并**保密处理**，报告者的隐私与安全是首要考虑。

维护者会依据 Contributor Covenant 的执行指南采取其认为适当的措施，最高可至
**临时或永久禁止参与本项目**。
